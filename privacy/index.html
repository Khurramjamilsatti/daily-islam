Daily Islam

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who this policy is for

Daily Islam is a free Islamic app and website for prayer times, the Quran, hadith, nasheeds, books, and daily learning. This policy explains what information the phone apps and this website use, and what stays on your device.

The publisher is Muhammad Khurram Jamil. You can write to khurram.jamilsatti@gmail.com. The same policy is published at khurram-jamil.gitlab.io/my-profile/daily-islam/privacy/.

The website and the phone apps

The website and the Android and iOS apps share the same reading content. They do not work the same way with your device. The phone apps can ask for location, show ads, use the camera for a barcode, and use the flashlight. This website shows one Google AdSense ad in the home page and one Google AdSense ad at the top of a detail page. The ads sit in the page, so you can keep reading. No other ads are loaded. Sexual content, nudity, and betting are not wanted on this site. It does not ask you to sign in.

Opening a page on the website sends the usual technical details that come with any visit, such as your IP address, the browser you use, and the page you requested. Daily Islam does not run its own account system on the website.

Location

The phone app asks for your location so it can calculate prayer times, show the qibla direction, and help you find nearby mosques. Prayer times are calculated on the device from that location and from the calculation method you choose.

Daily Islam does not upload a location history to its own server. If you open a mosque in Google Maps, that place is sent to Google as part of the maps link. You can refuse location permission. Prayer times then stay on the last place the app already has, or they wait until a location is available.

Advertising on the phone apps

The Android and iOS apps are supported by ads. They show banner and full-screen ads through Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK. AdMob uses the advertising ID, device details, IP address, and ad interactions to show and measure ads.

On Android 13 and later the app declares the advertising-ID permission so that identifier is available. Daily Islam does not receive the advertising ID on its own server and does not sell personal information.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your phone settings under Google, then Ads, or at adssettings.google.com. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. The website shows only Google AdSense: one ad in the home page and one ad at the top of a detail page. It does not load the phone AdMob ads or any other ad network.

Reading content from the internet

To show Quran text, translation, recitation, tafseer, hadith, and the Hijri calendar, the app and the website request that content from outside services. Those services include Al Quran Cloud, Quran.com, Aladhan, and a public hadith collection delivered through jsDelivr.

A request like that carries the usual technical information, including your IP address. Daily Islam does not add your name or an account ID to those requests, because there is no account.

Nasheeds

Nasheed audio is streamed. The files are not stored inside the app as a private copy of the catalog. When a track plays, the request goes to the host that serves that file, and that host sees the usual connection details such as an IP address.

The catalog itself, including titles and which tracks are offered, is part of the app. Choosing Urdu or Arabic, or searching a title, happens on your device.

Halal product lookup

Scan Halal lets you type a barcode or, on a phone, scan one. The barcode is sent to Open Food Facts so the app can read the product name and ingredients and then check them on your device.

Open Food Facts receives the barcode and the usual connection details. Daily Islam does not attach your name to that lookup. The camera, when it is used, stays on the phone for the scan.

What stays on your device

Favorites, notes, the last place you read, learning progress, prayer settings, the calculation method, and similar preferences are saved on your phone. They are not uploaded to a Daily Islam server.

Clearing the app’s storage, or deleting the app, removes those saved items from the phone. The website does not keep a copy of your notes or favorites on a server either.

What Daily Islam does not do

Daily Islam does not sell your personal information. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for a payment card. There is no Daily Islam login.

The app does not read your contacts, photos, or messages in order to build a profile. Permissions it does ask for, such as location, notifications, or the camera, are used for the feature in front of you.

Children

Daily Islam is a general-audience app and is supported by ads on the phone. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to the contact address, write to khurram.jamilsatti@gmail.com and ask for that message to be deleted.

Sharing from the app

When you tap share on a hadith or another passage, the text is handed to the share sheet on your device. Whatever app you then choose, such as a message or a social app, receives that text under its own rules. Daily Islam does not see who you sent it to.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page and on the published copy, with a new date at the top. Continuing to use Daily Islam after that date means the new text is the one that applies.

The date on this page is 10 November 2026. Check the published copy if you want to confirm you are reading the latest version.
